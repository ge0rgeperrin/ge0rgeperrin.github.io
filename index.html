PortSlop

A small personal Mac app that reposts my own TikTok videos to my own YouTube channel as Shorts.

1. WatchesChecks my public TikTok profile for new posts.
2. DownloadsSaves the new video temporarily on my Mac.
3. UploadsPosts it to my YouTube channel, then deletes the local copy.

PortSlop runs entirely on the user's own computer. It has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. It uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to the channel of the person who signed in, and to show that channel's name.

Read the Privacy Policy for exactly what data is accessed and how.
